Hi! I am Skye (she/they), welcome to my star-studded virtual home, tucked away in the coziest fringes of the great web beneath the deepest stellar ocean. Skye Journey is a personal, non-profit site, not only a space for self-expression but also a place to share and spread knowledge.

I'm an artist, a concept art illustrator and a developer, a student working toward a degree in astronomy, and a lover of philosophy. In my blogs you'll find me talking about personal things as much as about all kinds of topics, mostly from the world of physics, from quantum mechanics to astrophysics. Also security, art, or simply my opinions and philosophy.

This site contains javascript, iframes, and sound in autoplay! It's made for desktop (intended viewing size is 1080px), though some pages should work on mobile. If you are returning, please CTRL+F5 if anything looks broken! The site is still a work in progress, so it may have some bugs, and autoplay won't work the same on iOS devices.

quick advice about the site:

✦ There is no explicit content on my site, but please keep in mind that it is made by an adult and for an adult audience. So please consider this before entering or reading my personal journal.

✦ None of the assets used on this website belong to me. Please check the credits section!

✦ Make sure to allow music and sounds for a better experience on your browser.

Update log

Under-the-hood cleanup
09.sep.26

Reorganized the site's code from the inside. Nothing looks different, but everything is way tidier now: fonts in one place, shared components, zero jQuery, and I patched up a bunch of broken links I had lying around. Full story on the changelog!

Big website update
31.dec.24

The website is in the middle of a massive update! We will now have a new layout, a different background in the personal section, a day and night system, a notification system, a journal and photo gallery! I'm working hard on this update so that it will be fully released in the next few months.

Long time no see!
22.march.25

I'm back after a long period of work and rest! I'm working once again on developing the website, and I took my first step toward moving it from Neocities to my Github.

Back to Neocities
26.april.25

I just discovered that Neocities has an official, supported command line interface tool to upload your website. This is a good option if you want to decouple your site from GitHub! My website code will remain on GitHub, I'm still figuring out how to handle this and how it works, but I'm happy to bring my site back to Neocities for the 2.2 update!

Advice

I recommend browsing the page in an environment other than your phone. For technical support, use the nav bar on the left side! I know, is not perfect, but was made with love and care.
