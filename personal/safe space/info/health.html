Surviving psychosis and a traumatic event can be very hard and difficult to deal with. Usually, victims of accidents and abuse who survive carry enormous feelings of guilt, and I am one of them. I am grateful to my psychiatrist and my therapist for helping me deal with all this, they have helped me through these years of recovery. I am also grateful to the mental hospital that treated me during my moments of crisis.
