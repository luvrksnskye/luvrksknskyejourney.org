You have captured Altair! In Chinese and Japanese legends, Altair is known as Niulang (the Cowherd) in the tale of The Princess and the Shepherd. She is separated from her lover Vega by the "Celestial River" (the Milky Way), and they can only meet once a year, during the Star Festival (Tanabata). Also, in Greek mythology, Altair represents the eagle that carries Zeus's thunderbolts.
