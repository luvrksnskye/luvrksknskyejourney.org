Special Credits

Hello there. This is a list of people and resources that have contributed to the creation of this website, making assets or supporting its development somehow. Even though there are many people with who I am no longer in contact, I appreciate and give credit where it's due.

Please take a look at the list below to see all the amazing people and resources that have helped make this website possible! You can also find them in my resource page and the source code of the site, which you can access any time. Since the site is still in development, I may add more credits in the future and you may notice that I need to move some things around, including the files architecture of this big project.

Also, a lot of stuff I used for my site can be found on my resources page! I have done my best to seek credit for all of the resources I use on this page besides the ones I obtained myself by purchasing them for my personal use, on top of that if I have used anything that is not free to use (pixels and game assets) please contact me and I will gladly remove it! If you know of credit for something that you don't see credited or can't find what you're looking for, you can always send a message in my chat box or email. Happy coding!
