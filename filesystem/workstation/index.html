My Creative Space

Welcome to My Digital Sanctuary!

This is where magic happens--where I bring ideas to life through code, design, and art. I've carefully crafted this space to inspire creativity and keep me motivated during those long work sessions.

My philosophy: A tidy space equals a clear mind. I love keeping my workstation organized but with personal touches that make it feel like home.

Setup Details

I don't currently have a desk due to moving across the country, so I use my MacBook sometimes in bed, and when I need more comfort, at the kitchen table where I usually eat. I used to have a huge setup with plenty of space! But now I've decided to have a smaller room and explore the possibilities of arranging it to my liking.

I used to have a small and simple setup with my old laptop, using Arch Linux and then Debian. Now I just have my bed at this moment! Feel free to explore other sections to learn more about my tools and specifications! Each piece has been chosen with care and purpose. Remember you can also check the terminal!

My Creative Toolkit

My Digital Brush & Canvas

As both a developer and artist, I've curated a collection of tools that help me bring my visions to life. Here's what I use to create, design, and stay organized!

Development

VS Code - My code editor of choice with custom themes
Git - For version control and collaboration
Docker - Containerization for consistent environments
Godot - Game engine for 2D and 3D game development

Design & Art

Terminal Setup
